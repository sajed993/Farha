# فرحة Farha — Publication Plan

**Budget:** 0–500 TND cash over twelve months · **Time:** 15–25 h/week ·
**Markets:** Tunisia + diaspora (France, Italy) · **Real launch:** the 2027 season

Read [`farha-business-analysis.md`](./farha-business-analysis.md) first — it explains
why this plan starts in September and not today. The short version: the Tunisian
wedding season runs late May to September, invitations go out 4–8 weeks ahead, and it
is 8 August. **The selling months are April to July. We have eight months to prepare
for them, and that is a gift, not a delay.**

The plan has one organising idea. With no ad money, **the invitation itself is the
advertisement.** Every one you deliver is opened by 150–300 wedding-age people on
their phones. Everything below either feeds that loop or removes something blocking
it.

---

## Phase 0 — Repair · September–October 2026
**Spend: 0–50 TND · Time: ~40 h total · Goal: become sellable**

Nothing is published in this phase. Publishing before it is finished wastes the one
thing you can't buy back — the first impression on a small market.

| Action | Cost | Why it comes first |
|---|---|---|
| Re-score all 16 films with CC0 / royalty-free music | **0 TND** | Unlicensed audio is a legal exposure *and* gets your organic clips muted on TikTok and Instagram — it disables this entire plan |
| Add the brand mark + link to the guest invitation view | **0 TND** | Switches on the loop that every number in this plan depends on |
| Add a referral credit (couple gets 20 DT back per referral) | **0 TND** | Makes the loop measurable, and gives the couple a reason to name you |
| Integrate Konnect payments (1.3% local / 2.9% intl) | **0 TND** to register | Without a card, the diaspora cannot buy at all |
| Buy the domain | **~50 TND/yr** | No domain, no credibility. `.tn` or `.com` |
| Move phone / D17 / RIB out of the public repo | **0 TND** | Personal data, publicly indexed |
| Compress video, posters-first, Cloudflare CDN | **0 TND** | 42 MB of media on a 100 GB/month free tier fails on your best day |
| Create Instagram + TikTok accounts, claim the handles | **0 TND** | Do it before you need them |

**Then, still in Phase 0 — the eight seed invitations.** Give away eight Signature-
quality invitations, free, to real couples marrying between September and December
(winter weddings, engagements, henna nights). Choose them for their guest lists, not
their gratitude.

What you get back, and must ask for explicitly before you start:
1. Permission to photograph and film the invitation being opened at the event.
2. A written testimonial in Arabic and French.
3. Their guest list reach — 8 × ~200 = **~1,600 people see a Farha invitation**.
4. A portfolio of real Tunisian weddings, which is the trust signal you have none of.

**Exit criterion:** five blockers closed, domain live, eight invitations delivered,
eight testimonials and at least twenty usable photographs in hand.

## Phase 1 — Build the audience · November 2026–February 2027
**Spend: 0–100 TND · Time: ~6 h/week · Goal: 3,000 followers and 5 partners**

Now you publish. Off-season, so almost nothing sells — that is fine. You are
building the audience that will buy in April.

### Organic video — the main channel
You already own sixteen films. Each one cuts into six to ten vertical clips. **That is
over a hundred pieces of content you have already paid for.** Post 4–5 times a week,
consistently, on TikTok and Instagram Reels.

What works, in order:
1. **The wax seal opening.** Three seconds, no explanation. It's the hook.
2. **Before/after** — a plain printed card, then the film. The comparison sells itself.
3. **The guest's-eye view** — a phone in a hand at a real wedding, filmed at the seed
   events. This is why Phase 0 collects footage.
4. **Occasion-specific** — henna night, newborn, bac graduation. Each opens a
   different audience.
5. **Behind the scenes** — how a Signature film gets made. This is what sells the
   249 DT tier, which is where your margin is.

Caption in Arabic first, French second. Never post the price in the caption; put it in
the bio link. The question "بشحال؟" in the comments is engagement, and answering it
publicly is a conversation with the whole audience.

### Facebook groups — free, and where Tunisian commerce happens
Tunisian wedding, bride and marketplace groups run to millions of members. Join as a
person, not a brand. Answer questions about invitations for weeks before you ever
mention your own. One helpful post a week beats ten promotional ones, and groups ban
the latter.

### Vendor partnerships — paid in commission, never in cash
Approach **wedding photographers, halls, planners, makeup artists and florists**.
They already have the customer at exactly the moment the invitation decision is made.

Offer: **20% commission per referred sale** (≈20 DT on Collection, ≈50 DT on
Signature), paid after delivery, plus a free invitation for their own use so they
know what they're recommending. Target five to ten partners in Grand Tunis, Sousse and
Sfax. **Cost: 0 TND up front.** It only ever costs you money when it has already made
you money.

### Optional small spend
Up to 100 TND boosting the two best-performing organic posts. Boost what already
works; never boost a cold post.

**Exit criterion:** ~3,000 combined followers, 5+ active vendor partners, 8–11
orders/month, and a clear picture of which three content formats actually convert.

## Phase 2 — Pre-season · March–April 2027
**Spend: 100–200 TND · Time: ~10 h/week · Goal: fill the season calendar**

This is when couples marrying in June, July and August start deciding.

**Open the booking calendar publicly** and show it filling. Scarcity is honest here —
your delivery capacity is genuinely finite — and it is the most persuasive thing you
can say. "Three Signature slots left for July" is true and it works.

**Switch on the diaspora.** French-first content and a French-language landing route.
The message is specific to them: *your guests are in Paris, Lyon, Milan and Tunis at
once, and a printed card cannot reach all of them.* Konnect (from Phase 0) means they
can now actually pay. Target Tunisian community groups and pages in France and Italy —
organic, in groups, not with ads.

**Ramadan and Eid timing.** Engagements cluster around Eid; save-the-date invitations
are the natural product and the cheapest entry point into a customer you will sell
twice more before their wedding.

**Introduce the wedding package.** Save-the-date + henna + wedding as one price. This
is Lever 1 from the analysis and it is the largest revenue increase available to you
— roughly +40–60% with no new customers.

**Exit criterion:** 22+ orders in April, calendar visibly filling for June–July, at
least three package sales, first diaspora sale completed by card.

## Phase 3 — Peak · May–August 2027
**Spend: 200–250 TND · Time: 20–28 h/week · Goal: deliver without breaking**

The whole year happens here. Base case: 132 orders across four months, ~17,000 TND,
peaking at 38 orders in July.

**The main risk in this phase is not demand. It is you.** 38 orders is ~114 hours,
about 28 h/week — past the ceiling you set. Plan for it before it arrives:

- **Cap Signature at 6/month** and say so publicly. It protects delivery quality and
  the scarcity is real.
- **Templatise the intake.** One form, all three languages at once, no back-and-forth.
- **Stop selling when full.** A late invitation at a wedding is not a refund problem,
  it is a reputation problem, in a market where everyone knows everyone.
- **Raise prices before you raise hours.** If June sells out in the first week, the
  July price is too low. Going 99 → 119 DT on the Collection costs you the customers
  you could not have served anyway.

**Spend the remaining 200–250 TND here**, and only on retargeting people who already
viewed an invitation. Nothing else.

**Ask every delivered customer for one thing**: a photo or clip of the invitation on
someone's phone at their wedding. That is next season's content, and it is free.

**Exit criterion:** the season delivered, zero late invitations, 30+ testimonials, and
a rolling-twelve-month figure to judge the business by.

## What to measure, and when to change course

| Metric | Where from | Base-case target |
|---|---|---|
| Orders per month | dashboard | see forecast table |
| Blended average order value | dashboard revenue ÷ orders | 129 DT → 159 DT after packages |
| Invitation opens per order | `events` table (`schema-8-analytics.sql`) | 150+ |
| Site visits from invitation footer | UTM on the brand-mark link | **the number that proves the loop** |
| Referral credits claimed | referral feature | 1 per 6 invitations |
| Orders from vendor partners | order note field | 20% of total by Phase 3 |
| Diaspora orders (international card) | Konnect | 15% of total by Phase 3 |

**Three decision points:**

- **End of Phase 1 (Feb 2027).** If invitation-footer traffic is near zero, the loop
  is not firing. Diagnose it before spending a dinar on Phase 2 — everything downstream
  assumes it works.
- **End of Phase 2 (Apr 2027).** If April is under 9 orders you are tracking the
  conservative case. Cut the ad spend to zero, put everything into vendor partnerships,
  and treat 2027 as a portfolio-building year.
- **September 2027.** Rolling twelve months over ~20,000 TND → build the self-serve
  editor and break the labour ceiling. Under ~8,000 TND → keep Farha as a referral-only
  boutique service, which at 96% margin is still worth running.
